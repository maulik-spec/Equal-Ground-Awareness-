Legal protections depend on the facts, age, location and the law in force. Get advice from a qualified Indian lawyer or legal-aid service about your situation.

01 India’s child-protection laws include gender-specific provisions; the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 is framed to protect children regardless of sex.

02 Reporting duties and procedures can apply in child sexual-offence cases. A qualified lawyer or child-protection professional can explain what to do safely.

03 The law changes. This site does not interpret a case, guarantee an outcome, or replace official sources and legal counsel.
